# Staff guide

Welcome to the Feelings team. This guide shows you how to use the Feelings staff app on your phone: how to put it on your phone, how to join, how to check in and out, and how to ask for leave and see your payslip.

If you get stuck at any point, ask Urmi. Every screen in the app has a way to reach her.

## What the app does

- You check in when you reach the salon and check out when you leave. The app writes down the time.
- You can see your days, your leave and your payslips.
- You can ask Urmi for leave, or ask her to fix a day when something went wrong.

**What the app never does:**

- It never follows you. The app looks at your location **only at the moment you tap Check in or Check out**, to see that you are at the salon. It does not look at any other time, and it does not keep a map of where you go.
- It does not give you scores, streaks or a "late" shaming screen. It does not compare you with other people.
- Nothing is taken from your salary because of a warning on a check-in. Only Urmi decides about your pay, and the app shows every line of how your pay is worked out.

We keep the exact location of a check-in for 90 days and a selfie (only if Urmi has turned selfies on) for 30 days. After that they are deleted. Your wage records are kept for 5 years, because the law says the salon must.

## Put the app on your phone

Urmi will send you a WhatsApp message with a link and a join code. Open the link on your phone. The page shows you how to add the app to your home screen. You must do this first. The app does not work inside the browser.

<!-- screenshot: staff-invite-page -->

### Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge)

1. Open the link from Urmi's message.
2. Tap **Install the app**.
3. When it says "Done", close the browser and open **Feelings** from your home screen.

If the link opened inside WhatsApp and there is no Install button, tap the three dots and choose **Open in Chrome**, then try again.

<!-- screenshot: staff-install-android -->

**One setting that helps** on some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung): these phones put Chrome to sleep and alerts come late. Set Chrome's battery use to **No restrictions**. The app shows you where, once, after you install it.

### iPhone

You must use **Safari**. Other browsers, and the browser inside WhatsApp, cannot add the app.

1. Open the link in Safari. If it opened somewhere else, tap **Copy link**, open Safari and paste it.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow).
3. Scroll and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Feelings** from your home screen.

<!-- screenshot: staff-install-iphone -->

Write down the join code now, or tap **Copy**. You will type it inside the app in the next step.

### On a computer

The page asks you to open the link on your phone. Scan the code on the page with your phone camera.

## Join with your code

The first time you open the app, you will see **Join your team**. This takes about two minutes.

1. **Phone number and code.** Type your mobile number and the join code from Urmi's message (it looks like `K7M-4QPX`). The code works once and for 48 hours. On some Android phones it is already filled in.
   - If it says "That code doesn't match this number", check both and try again. After 5 wrong tries you must wait an hour, or ask Urmi for a new invite.
2. **Privacy notice.** A short page in plain words tells you what we keep and why. Read it, then tap **I understand**. If Urmi has turned on selfies, there is a second, separate question: "Take a photo when you check in". You choose; you can change your mind later in **Me**.
3. **Choose your PIN.** 4 to 6 numbers, typed twice. Choose something only you know.
   - The app says "Pick a PIN that's harder to guess" for numbers like 1111, 1234, 0000, or the last digits of your phone number.
   - You will type this PIN every time you check in. Do not tell anyone, and do not write it where others can see it.
4. **Notifications.** Tap **Turn on notifications**. You get a ping when Urmi approves your phone, your leave or your payslip. On iPhone this is needed for pings to work at all. You can tap **Not now** and turn it on later in **Me**.
5. **Wait for Urmi.** The screen says "Urmi needs to approve this phone before you can check in." We have told her. The screen updates by itself when she approves, and you also get a ping.

<!-- screenshot: staff-join-steps -->

If Urmi does not approve your phone, the screen says so. Talk to her, then she can send you a new invite.

Each person can have **one** approved phone. If you join on a second phone, it replaces the first one after Urmi approves it.

## Check in and check out

The **Today** screen has one big red button. It says **Check in**. After you check in, it says **Check out**.

<!-- screenshot: staff-today -->

### Checking in

1. Open the app and tap **Check in**.
2. The app finds your location. You see words like "Finding you..." and then "Got it: at the salon". Stand near a window or the door if it takes long.
3. If Urmi has turned selfies on, take a picture of your face, in good light.
4. Type your PIN.
5. You see "You're in at 10:02." The time is the time on the salon's server, not on your phone.

The whole thing should take less than ten seconds.

<!-- screenshot: staff-checkin-success -->

You can check in from one hour before your shift starts. Before that, the app says "Your shift starts at 10:00. You can check in from 9:00." You can still choose **Check in anyway**, and Urmi decides.

### Checking out

Same steps, no selfie. You see "You're out at 20:07. 9 h 05 m today." If you stay 30 minutes or more after your shift, it adds "Stayed 37 min after your shift. Urmi will see this as overtime to approve."

### On a week off, a holiday or a leave day

The app asks before it lets you check in: "Today is your week off. Check in anyway? Urmi will see it as extra work." If you are on leave, checking in cancels that day's leave. Say yes only if you really are working.

### If you forget to check out

About 30 minutes after your shift ends, you get "Still at work? Check out when you leave." If you never check out, the app closes your day at the end of your shift, tells you ("We closed your day at 20:00"), and gives you a **Fix the time** button if you stayed longer.

## What the messages mean

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Finding you... 42 m accuracy" | The app is looking for your location. The number is how exact it is. | Wait a few seconds. Step near a window or the door. |
| "Got it: at the salon" | You are inside the salon area. | Carry on. |
| "You're about 340 m from the salon." | You are too far away to check in. | Walk to the salon, or use **Scan door code**, **I'm at a venue** or **Send to Urmi**. |
| "Location is off for this app." | The phone is not letting the app see your location. | Follow the steps on the screen (Android: lock icon, Permissions, Location, Allow, Precise. iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Location Services, Safari Websites, While Using, Precise on). Then tap **Try again**. |
| "Your phone is sharing only an approximate location. Turn on Precise location for this app." | Your phone gives a rough location only. | Turn on **Precise** location for the app, then **Try again**. |
| "We couldn't find your location. Step near a window or the door and try again." | No location in 20 seconds. | **Try again**, or **Scan door code**, or **Send to Urmi**. |
| "Wrong PIN. 3 tries left." | The PIN is wrong. | Type it again. |
| "Too many wrong tries. Try again at 10:42." | Five wrong PINs. The app waits 15 minutes. | Wait, or tap **Send to Urmi** with a note. |
| "We couldn't confirm you're at the salon. Scan the door code, or send this to Urmi." | The app is not sure about the check-in. Your time is kept. | Scan the door code (next section), or tap **Send to Urmi**. |
| "We couldn't reach the server. Your check-in is saved and will send by itself." | No internet. | See [No internet](#no-internet). |
| "Your check-in at 10:04 was approved" | Urmi checked it. | Nothing. |
| "Urmi didn't accept your 10:04 check-in" | She did not accept it, and gives a reason. | Talk to her, or use [Fix a day](#fix-a-day). |
| "Urmi hasn't approved this phone yet." | Your phone is waiting for approval. | Ask Urmi. |
| "This phone was removed. Ask Urmi." | Your phone was taken off the list. | Ask Urmi. |
| "Please update the app." | There is a new version. | Tap **Update now**. |
| "A new version is ready." | Same. | Tap **Update**. |
| "Your account is not active. Talk to Urmi." | Your account is not active. | Talk to Urmi. |

A check-in that is flagged is **never** a reason for a cut in your pay. If you did nothing wrong, Urmi just looks and clears it. Your check-in still counts as normal until she says otherwise.

## The door code

If your phone cannot confirm where you are, or Urmi has asked you to use the door code, scan it:

1. Tap **Scan door code**.
2. Point your camera at the screen at the door (or at Urmi's phone if she is showing the code).
3. The code changes every 30 seconds, so scan it right away.

You still type your PIN. The code is only extra proof. If you open the code with your normal phone camera, it only shows a page that says "Open Feelings and tap Scan door code". It does not check you in.

If the app says "That code has changed. Scan the new one", scan again. "That's not this salon's code" or "That's not the door code" means you scanned the wrong thing. If the camera is off, follow the steps on the screen, or tap **Send to Urmi**.

## Wedding and venue days

When you work at a wedding or another venue, Urmi sets up the venue in the app.

- You get a ping: "Venue duty tomorrow 04:00-09:00 at Hotel Aram." The duty shows on **Today** and in **Month**.
- At the venue, check in the same way. The app checks that you are at the venue, not the salon.
- Hours outside your normal shift on a venue duty count as overtime that Urmi has already agreed to, if she left that setting on.

If no duty was set up, and you reach the venue: when the app says you are outside the salon, tap **I'm at a venue**. Type the place name (for example "Patel wedding, Lal Bungalow"), add a note, and send. Urmi gets a message and decides. Your time is kept from the first moment you tapped.

If you work before 6 in the morning or after 9 at night, the app may ask you once to say that you agree to it.

## No internet

If there is no internet, the check-in is saved on your phone. You see "1 check-in waiting to send" and "In at 10:02 (waiting to send)". Open the app once you have signal and it sends by itself. The app checks again every 30 seconds while it is open. (iPhone cannot send in the background, so open the app.)

If the check-in cannot be sent (for example because your phone was removed meanwhile), the banner says "One check-in couldn't be sent. Tap to see why." and offers **Send to Urmi**.

A check-in that was saved for many hours may need Urmi's approval. The time still counts from when you tapped.

## Your month

**Month** shows your calendar. Each day has a letter and a picture, not only a colour: present, half day, absent, leave, week off or holiday. At the top you see your totals for the month: days present, late minutes and overtime that Urmi approved.

Tap a day to see what happened: your check-in and check-out times, where, the shift, hours worked, and what it does to your pay, in words and in rupees. For example "Late by 25 min. 10 min grace, so 15 min count." If Urmi changed something, it says "Edited by Urmi".

## Ask for leave

**Requests**, then **Ask for leave**.

1. Choose the type. You see how many days you have left, for example "Casual 5 left", "Sick 7 left".
2. Choose the dates. Week offs and holidays inside your dates are shown and not counted.
3. Write a reason. For unpaid leave you must write one. Up to 300 letters.
4. Read the notes the app shows before you send:
   - Casual leave needs 2 days' notice. Sick leave can be the same day, or up to 3 days back.
   - If you ask for more days than you have, it says "You have 2 casual leaves left. The other 1 day will be unpaid." You choose.
   - If the days are in a busy period it says "26 Oct is a no-leave day (wedding season). Urmi may say no." You can still send.
   - If you already asked for that day, it says "You already asked for 26 Oct."
5. Tap **Send**.

You get a ping when Urmi decides. If you change your mind before she decides, you can cancel. If it was approved, you can still cancel days that have not come yet, and Urmi is told. If the month is closed, the app says "March is closed. Ask Urmi."

## Fix a day

If you forgot to check in or out, or the time is wrong, ask Urmi to fix it.

1. **Month**, tap the day, then **Something's wrong with this day**. (Or tap **Fix the time** in the ping you got.)
2. Choose what happened: forgot to check in, forgot to check out, wrong time, or worked at a venue without a duty.
3. Enter the right time and write what happened. Be honest and short.
4. Tap **Send**.

You can fix days from the last 7 days, in a month that is not closed, and one fix at a time for a day. A fix does not give you overtime by itself; overtime is a separate decision.

## Advances

If Urmi records an advance for you, you get a message such as "Urmi recorded an advance of ₹3,000 on 12 Oct. Recovery: ₹3,000 from October salary." Tap **That's right**, or **Something's wrong** to tell her. Your payslip shows what was taken back each month.

## Your payslip

After Urmi finishes the month, you get "Your October payslip is ready".

1. **Payslips**, tap the month.
2. The payslip shows every line and how it was worked out, for example "₹15,000 / 31 days x 29 paid days".
3. **Download PDF** saves it. **Share** sends it, for example to your family on WhatsApp.
4. The PDF has a small code. Anyone who scans it sees only your first name's initial, the month, the amount and the version. Nothing else.

If the PDF says "Preparing the PDF...", wait a minute. The online payslip is always there.

If you think a line is wrong, talk to Urmi. Every number comes from your check-ins, so you can see why.

## Me: your phone, language and data

The **Me** tab has:

- **Language:** English or Gujarati.
- **Notifications:** on or off, and a help text if they are off.
- **This phone:** the name of your phone and the day Urmi approved it.
- **My data:** what we keep about you: your check-ins and whether each was inside, near the edge or outside, any selfies still kept (with the date each is deleted), and what you agreed to. You can **Ask for a correction**, and see Urmi's contact for any complaint.
- **Privacy notice:** the full text.
- **Sign out.**

If you turned on selfies and later change your mind, you can withdraw the selfie permission here. Your check-ins then go to Urmi to approve until she turns selfies off for you.

If you leave the salon, your phone number, photo and selfies are deleted within 30 days, and exact locations after 90 days. Your wage records stay for 5 years, as the law says.

## New phone, forgot PIN

- **New phone:** open the app on the new phone, tap **I have a new phone**, and type your phone number and your PIN. Urmi gets a message to approve it. You can check in once she does. If you cannot remember your PIN, see below.
- **Forgot your PIN:** ask Urmi to reset it. She sends you a new code on WhatsApp. Type it to set a new PIN. Your phone stays approved.
- **Wrong PIN many times:** after 5 wrong tries the app waits 15 minutes. Urmi is told if it happens many times in a day, in case someone else is trying.
- **If you clear your browser data or remove the app**, the phone's key is lost, and Urmi must approve the phone again. Keep the app on your home screen, and do not clear data for it.
- **The app asks you to sign in again** after about a month of not using it: type your phone number and PIN.

## Questions

**Why does the app need my location?** To see that you are at the salon when you check in. Only then.

**Can Urmi see where I am during the day?** No. The app does not read your location at any other time.

**My phone is slow or old. Will it work?** Yes. Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone and keep them updated.

**What if I check in by mistake?** Tell Urmi. She can fix the day.

**Who do I ask?** Urmi. Every error screen has a way to reach her.
